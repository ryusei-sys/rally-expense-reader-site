Disconnect access

  1. Open Rally Essentials in QuickBooks Online.
  2. Open its connected-apps management page.
  3. Select Rally Expense Reader and disconnect it.
  4. Ask the company administrator to remove its locally stored credentials and any retained review files as needed.

Disconnecting this app does not delete or change your QuickBooks transactions.
