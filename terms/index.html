Internal-use terms

Rally Expense Reader is provided for Rally Essentials’ authorized company administrator to review its own accounting information. It is not offered for use by outside customers.

The tool retrieves accounting information for review. It does not change the books, make payments or cancel subscriptions.

Reports may contain incomplete classifications or suspected duplicate charges. The owner verifies underlying transactions and actual product usage before any bookkeeping or cancellation decision. This tool does not replace the company’s accountant or tax adviser.

Use remains subject to the company’s applicable Intuit and OpenAI arrangements. Access must remain limited to authorized company personnel. API credentials must not be shared in chat, public repositories or reports.

Publication of these internal-use terms is not a representation that Intuit has approved production access.
