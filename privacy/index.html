Privacy notice

Rally Expense Reader is private internal software operated by Rally Essentials to review its own bookkeeping expenses. It is not a public multi-customer service.

Information and purpose

After an owner-authorized QuickBooks connection, the local connector can read company, vendor, purchase, bill, credit, account and general-ledger records for internal expense analysis. Exposed tools cannot create, edit, pay or delete accounting transactions.

Credentials and storage

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on the company Mac Mini using AES-256-GCM encryption with a separate owner-restricted key file. Credentials are not included in analysis output. The public information site does not receive accounting records or credentials.

AI-assisted review

The owner may direct authorized record summaries into its OpenAI Codex/ChatGPT session for expense analysis. The connector does not train or fine-tune models, sell data, use data for advertising, or grant access to another customer account. OpenAI processing and retention are governed by the applicable account agreement and settings; these must be reviewed before production use. This notice does not represent Intuit approval of that processing.

Retention and disconnection

Credentials remain local while the connection is active. The administrator can revoke access from QuickBooks’ connected-apps settings and request removal of local credentials. Any downloaded review files remain subject to the owner’s retention choices. No permanent transaction archive or automated sending schedule is installed by this setup.

Access

Access is limited to authorized company administration. Contact the company at the address below to request access removal or ask about this notice.
